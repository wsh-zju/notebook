## 函数的增长
1. $f = O(g(x))$:$|f(x)| \leq C|g(x)|$

2. $f = \Omega(g(x))$:$|f(x)| \geq C|g(x)|$

3. $f = \Theta(g(x))$:$f = O(g(x))$ 且 $f = \Omega(g(x))$
